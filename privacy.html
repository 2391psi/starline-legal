This Privacy Policy ("Policy") describes what personal data the Starline media-player application ("the App") and its trial/license service process, why, and what rights you have.

Effective date: 8 October 2026 · Version 2026-10-08

1. WHO IS RESPONSIBLE

The App is published by the Starline developer ("the Developer"), who is the controller for the data processed by the trial/license service described in Section 6. Contact for all privacy questions and requests: contact@starlineplayer.com (support: starline.support@gmail.com). Starline is a media player: it does not provide, host or sell films, programmes or other media content.

2. DATA STORED ONLY ON YOUR DEVICE

The App stores the following data in the local storage of your device. The Developer does not receive it:

  • addresses and credentials of SMB, NFS and WebDAV servers you add (credentials are kept in Android Keystore-backed encrypted storage);
  • media library records, playback history, resume positions and the titles you edit;
  • technical details of media files (codec, resolution, HDR format, audio and subtitle tracks), read from file headers, including files on network sources, and used only to show format information in the library; media files are neither modified nor uploaded;
  • App settings (interface language, subtitle appearance, buffering and other preferences);
  • downloaded metadata and cached artwork;
  • locally generated diagnostic logs (release builds do not keep detailed debug logs).

Cloud backup and device-to-device transfer of App data are disabled. Uninstalling the App deletes this local data. It does not delete data held by the license service, the stores or TheTVDB.

3. DATA THE APP DOES NOT COLLECT

The App contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDK. It does not collect usage statistics, does not sell or share data for advertising, and does not access your contacts, location, camera or microphone. Your server credentials are sent only to the server you choose to connect to, never to the Developer, TheTVDB, Google or Amazon.

4. NETWORK CONNECTIONS AND DISCOVERY

The App connects directly to the SMB, NFS, WebDAV, HTTP and HTTPS resources you select; playback traffic does not pass through the Developer's servers. When you start a server search, the App uses local-network discovery (multicast), which other devices on the network can observe. Unencrypted protocols (plain HTTP, SMB without encryption, NFS) can be observed by the network operator and other parties on that network; prefer HTTPS where possible. The operator of a server you add is responsible for its own processing.

5. THETVDB

To identify your media, the App sends TheTVDB (operated by Whip Networks, Inc., United States) a title taken from a file name or entered by you, the year and media type when available, the selected metadata language and, necessarily, your IP address. Poster, backdrop and other images are loaded from TheTVDB's servers, which therefore also see your IP address. The App does not send media files, playback history, server credentials or payment information to TheTVDB. Metadata returned (titles, descriptions, genres, runtime, credits, seasons, episodes, ratings, images) is cached on your device. TheTVDB's own handling of data is described at https://thetvdb.com/privacy-policy. Metadata provided by TheTVDB (https://thetvdb.com). Please consider adding missing information or subscribing.

6. TRIAL AND LICENSE SERVICE

To prevent repeated use of the free trial, to authenticate genuine App builds and to verify purchases, the App communicates with the Developer's license service over HTTPS. For the Google Play version it sends the app-scoped Android ID; for the Amazon version, the Amazon user ID. Enrollment also sends the app name, store, package name and the SHA-256 hash of the signing certificate; the service issues a per-installation client ID and secret that are kept in encrypted storage on your device, and requests are signed and protected against replay with timestamps and one-time values.

The trial record is stored under a keyed cryptographic pseudonym (HMAC-SHA-256) of the platform identifier, together with the trial start date; the raw identifier is not stored in the trial table. Authentication records and purchase-verification records use a SHA-256 hash of the identifier. These are pseudonymous identifiers: they remain personal data and are not anonymous. For Amazon purchases the App also sends the product ID and receipt ID, which the service forwards to Amazon's Receipt Verification Service; the service stores a hash of the receipt, the product ID and the verification time. Google and Amazon process the purchase and payment under their own privacy terms; the Developer does not receive your name, email address or card details.

Legal bases (GDPR): purchase and entitlement verification - Article 6(1)(b) (performance of the purchase/license contract); app authentication, replay prevention and prevention of trial abuse - Article 6(1)(f) (legitimate interest in service security and fraud prevention). Providing the identifier is required to activate the trial or paid access; without it the service cannot verify entitlement. After successful verification the entitlement is stored locally, and normal launches need neither the service nor an Internet connection.

7. RETENTION

  • enrollment challenges: valid for two minutes, then deleted;
  • one-time request values (nonces): deleted after the five-minute authentication window;
  • authentication clients: deleted after 730 days without use;
  • trial records: deleted 730 days after the trial start;
  • purchase-verification records: deleted 730 days after the last verification;
  • privacy-request correspondence: kept only as long as needed to handle the request and demonstrate compliance.

The license service itself keeps no access log. Network infrastructure in front of it may process IP addresses temporarily for delivery and security. When a statutory retention duty applies, deletion may be limited accordingly.

Покупку и оплату обрабатывают Google и Amazon по своим условиям; Разработчик не получает ваше имя, адрес электронной почты или данные карты.
