This Privacy Policy ("Policy") describes what personal data the Starline media-player application ("the App") and its trial/license service process, why, and what rights you have.

Effective date: 16 October 2026 · Version 2026-10-16

1. WHO IS RESPONSIBLE

The App is published by the Starline developer ("the Developer"), who is the controller for the data processed by the trial/license service described in Section 6. Contact for all privacy questions and requests: contact@starlineplayer.com (support: starline.support@gmail.com). Starline is a media player: it does not provide, host or sell films, programmes or other media content.

2. DATA STORED ONLY ON YOUR DEVICE

The App stores the following data in the local storage of your device. The Developer does not receive it:

  • addresses and credentials of SMB, NFS and WebDAV servers you add (credentials are kept in Android Keystore-backed encrypted storage);
  • media library records, playback history, resume positions ("continue watching"), favourites, your own collections and the titles you edit;
  • technical details of media files (codec, resolution, HDR format, audio and subtitle tracks), read from file headers, including files on network sources, and used only to show format information in the library; media files are neither modified nor uploaded;
  • App settings (interface language, subtitle appearance, buffering and other preferences);
  • downloaded metadata and cached artwork;
  • the trial start date and the result of the last purchase check;
  • locally generated diagnostic logs (release builds do not keep detailed debug logs).

Google Play version on Android TV only: where the device supports it, the App shows a Starline channel and "Watch Next" entries on the Android TV home screen. For this, the titles and artwork of a small selection of library items and, for "Watch Next", the playback position are passed to the Android TV system on your device, which stores and displays them. The other versions do not use this function.

Cloud backup and device-to-device transfer of App data are disabled. Uninstalling the App deletes this local data. It does not delete data held by the license service, the stores or TheTVDB.

3. DATA THE APP DOES NOT COLLECT

The Developer has not integrated any analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDK into the App. The App does not collect usage statistics, does not sell or share data for advertising, and does not access your contacts, location, camera or microphone. Your server credentials are sent only to the server you choose to connect to, never to the Developer, TheTVDB, Google, Amazon or Huawei. Each store version contains only the payment SDK of its own store (Section 6); these SDKs are provided by the respective store, which processes data under its own terms. In the Huawei version, the HUAWEI IAP SDK includes HMS Core components that, according to Huawei, may send Huawei technical information about the use of the SDK (for example the SDK version and the results of SDK calls) for service operation and quality; this is not used by the Developer and is governed by Huawei's privacy statement (Section 6).

4. NETWORK CONNECTIONS AND DISCOVERY

The App connects directly to the SMB, NFS, WebDAV, HTTP and HTTPS resources you select; playback traffic does not pass through the Developer's servers. When you start a server search, the App uses local-network discovery (multicast), which other devices on the network can observe. Unencrypted protocols (plain HTTP, SMB without encryption, NFS) can be observed by the network operator and other parties on that network; prefer HTTPS where possible. The operator of a server you add is responsible for its own processing.

5. THETVDB

To identify your media, the App sends TheTVDB (operated by Whip Networks, Inc., United States) a title taken from a file name or entered by you, the year and media type when available, the selected metadata language and, necessarily, your IP address. Poster, backdrop and other images are loaded from TheTVDB's servers, which therefore also see your IP address. The App does not send media files, playback history, server credentials or payment information to TheTVDB. Metadata returned (titles, descriptions, genres, runtime, credits, seasons, episodes, ratings, images) is cached on your device. TheTVDB's own handling of data is described at https://thetvdb.com/privacy-policy. Metadata provided by TheTVDB (https://thetvdb.com). Please consider adding missing information or subscribing.

6. TRIAL AND LICENSE SERVICE

The App can be used free of charge for 7 days from the first playback. After that, playback requires a one-time in-app purchase of the full version through the store from which you obtained the App. To prevent repeated use of the free trial and to authenticate genuine App builds, the App communicates with the Developer's license service over HTTPS. It sends a platform identifier: in the Google Play and Huawei AppGallery versions, the Android ID (an identifier that Android generates separately for each app signing key, user and device); in the Amazon version, the Amazon user ID provided by the Amazon Appstore. Enrollment also sends the app name, the store version, the package name and the SHA-256 hash of the signing certificate; the service issues a per-installation client ID and secret that are kept in encrypted storage on your device, and requests are signed and protected against replay with timestamps and one-time values. The trial is checked at the first launch and recorded when it starts; after that the App normally does not contact the service again.

The trial record is stored under a keyed cryptographic pseudonym (HMAC-SHA-256) of the platform identifier, together with the trial start date; the raw identifier is not stored in the trial table, and the trial record is not linked to a particular store. Authentication records and purchase-verification records use a SHA-256 hash of the identifier. These are pseudonymous identifiers: they remain personal data and are not anonymous.

Purchases are processed by the store from which you obtained the App:

  • Google Play: the purchase is made through Google Play Billing. The App receives the purchase information from Google Play on your device and checks the product and the purchase state. It then sends the Android ID, the product ID and the purchase token to the license service, which confirms the purchase with the Google Play Developer API; unless the license service rejects the purchase, the App then confirms (acknowledges) it to Google Play. The service stores a hash of the purchase token, a hash of the order ID, the product ID, the purchase state, whether it is a test purchase, a hash of the Android ID of each device that uses the purchase and the verification times. One purchase can activate at most 5 new devices within any 30 days; devices replaced or reset over time do not use up the purchase. If the license service cannot be reached, access is granted provisionally for up to 72 hours until the purchase is confirmed.
  • Amazon Appstore: the purchase is made through Amazon In-App Purchasing. The App sends the Amazon user ID, the product ID and the receipt ID to the license service, which forwards them to Amazon's Receipt Verification Service; the service stores a hash of the user ID, a hash of the receipt, the product ID and the verification time.
  • Huawei AppGallery: the purchase is made through HUAWEI In-App Purchases (IAP); signing in with a HUAWEI ID may be required. The App receives purchase data signed by Huawei (including the product ID, order and purchase details, the purchase state and a purchase token) and checks the signature on your device. It then sends the Android ID, the product ID, the purchase token and the account type flag to the license service, which confirms the purchase with Huawei's order service. The service stores a hash of the purchase token, a hash of the order ID, the product ID, the purchase state, whether it is a test purchase, a hash of the Android ID of each device that uses the purchase and the verification times. One purchase can activate at most 5 new devices within any 30 days; devices replaced or reset over time do not use up the purchase. If the license service cannot be reached, access is granted provisionally for up to 72 hours until the purchase is confirmed.

Google, Amazon and Huawei process the purchase and payment under their own privacy terms; the Developer does not receive your name, email address or card details. The Huawei version includes the HUAWEI IAP SDK (HMS Core SDK) provided by Huawei, which communicates with HMS Core and AppGallery on your device. According to Huawei, HUAWEI IAP processes transaction information and, for payment, HUAWEI ID account data and device and network data such as the IP address: https://consumer.huawei.com/minisite/cloudservice/iap/common/b0/latest/privacy-statement.htm

The full version is a one-time, non-consumable purchase, not a subscription. After a successful check the entitlement is stored locally, so normal launches need neither the license service nor an Internet connection. "Restore purchase" asks the store for the purchases of the store account signed in on the device. In the Google Play and Huawei AppGallery versions, the license service also checks the store's list of cancelled and refunded purchases regularly and removes access for them. A purchase made in one store does not unlock a version obtained from another store; the license service does not transfer purchases between stores. If the store reports a purchase as cancelled or refunded, access may be removed at the next check.

Legal bases (GDPR): purchase and entitlement verification - Article 6(1)(b) (performance of the purchase/license contract); app authentication, replay prevention, prevention of trial abuse and limiting the number of new devices per Google Play or Huawei purchase - Article 6(1)(f) (legitimate interest in service security and fraud prevention). The identifier is needed for the trial check and for verifying paid access; without it the service cannot check the trial or verify an Amazon, Google Play or Huawei purchase.

Записи аутентификации и проверки покупок используют хэш SHA-256 идентификатора. Это псевдонимные идентификаторы: они остаются персональными данными и не являются анонимными.
